About me

I'm Stephen Schab, founder of Liberation Business Consulting & Development. I help small and mid-sized business owners get their time back by finding the repetitive work in their day and taking it off their plate.

I grew up in a family of engineers and entrepreneurs, watching my family build several successful businesses. The engineers taught me to build things that actually work. The entrepreneurs taught me what it's like to be the owner, where every hour and every dollar counts. Today I'm studying that side of business more formally as an Entrepreneurial MBA student.

My bachelor's degree, a double major in Accounting and Business Intelligence, taught me the language of business, so I can talk with you in your terms instead of tech jargon. My Risk Assurance & Advisory (Internal Audit) certification taught me to walk through a process step by step, spot where time and effort are slipping away, and figure out what's worth improving.

Here's how I work. We start by mapping how things get done today. Then I show you where the biggest gains are and recommend what fits your business best, whether that's a small tweak or a fully tailored system. I explain everything in plain language, and I'm here if you need help down the road.
